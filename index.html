A personal publishing tool for my own short-video channels

Reels Factory is a private, non-commercial desktop application operated by its developer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Helps me write scripts for original short videos (news digests, and narrated stories retold in my own words) and renders them with voice-over and subtitles on my own computer.
  • Uploads the finished videos to my own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), after I sign in with my Google account through Google OAuth.
  • Reads view, like and comment counts of my own uploaded videos (videos.list) so I can see which videos perform better.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any other person's YouTube account or data.
  • It does not download, scrape or re-upload other creators' videos through the YouTube API.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not show advertising.

By using this application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Personal data is handled as described in our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
